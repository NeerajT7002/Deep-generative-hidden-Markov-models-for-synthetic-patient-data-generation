STEP 1

Discrete Health State (St)

A 4-state Markov chain models whether the ICU patient is in Stable, Moderate, Guarded, or Critical condition each hour.

→
STEP 2

Continuous Latent Sampling (zt)

Within that state, continuous 2D coordinates are sampled via zt = μt + ε ⋅ σt to inject realistic physiological variance.

→
STEP 3

Neural Emission & Vitals Decoding (xt)

A deep multi-layer neural network decodes zt into the 6 continuous vital signs (Heart Rate, Blood Pressures, SpO₂, Respiration, Temp).

Trajectory: Hour 1 → Hour 24
State: Active Monitoring
State 0: Stable

Hemodynamically stable. HR 60-85 bpm, SBP 115-125 mmHg, SpO₂ > 98%.

State 1: Moderate

Mild distress or compensated tachycardia. HR 85-105 bpm, mild hypoxemia.

State 2: Guarded

Worsening vitals. HR 105-125 bpm, hypotension trend, high sepsis alert.

State 3: Critical

Acute decompensation / septic shock. Severe hypotension, multi-organ risk.
